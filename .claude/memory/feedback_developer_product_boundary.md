# Developer workflow is not product UX

Angela clarified this on 2026-09-27: she is the developer using this machine;
the worldwide audience for her products is distinct. Apply sound product and
engineering judgment. Do not turn her development diagnostics or preferences
into end-user requirements.

Her visible-command/test/browser rule governs development and verification.
The direct chat Whisperer is an internal product service: no extra console,
no focus activation and no visibility gate. Keep recording state in chat and
diagnostics in the main console/log. The normal application console remains.

The microphone beside Send starts Whisperer directly, gates capture, recognizes
and sends through the existing form. It is distinct from model-mediated
VOICE COMMANDS catalog workflows. Contract: docs/chat-microphone-design.md.
